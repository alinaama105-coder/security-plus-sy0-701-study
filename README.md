Study notes and progress while preparing for the CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 exam.

Status: In progress — not yet certified.

---

Exam Domains

- General Security Concepts
- Threats, Vulnerabilities and Mitigations
- Security Architecture
- Security Operations
- Security Program Management and Oversight

---

Topics I Am Reviewing

Security concepts

CIA triad, least privilege, defence in depth, Zero Trust, encryption, hashing, digital signatures and PKI.

Threats and vulnerabilities

Phishing, social engineering, malware, ransomware, credential attacks, brute force, vulnerability exploitation, insider threats and web attacks.

Identity and access

Authentication, authorisation, MFA, SSO, RBAC, privileged accounts and account lifecycle.

Network and architecture

Firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPNs, segmentation, secure protocols, cloud environments, virtualisation, redundancy and data protection.

Security operations

SIEM, log analysis, alert triage, incident response, vulnerability management, threat intelligence, endpoint monitoring and identity monitoring.

Governance and risk

Policies, procedures, risk assessment, compliance, awareness, business continuity, disaster recovery and third-party risk.
